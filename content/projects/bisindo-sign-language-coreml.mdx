# Storytelling: Bisindo Sign Language App

For the past few weeks, my team and I have been diving deep into Apple’s ecosystem to expand our technical understanding. The initial concept was simple: explore Apple frameworks and hardware by experimenting with iPhones, iPads, and Apple Watches.

We explored **CoreML**, **AVFoundation**, and **Foundation Models** to build a sign language app designed to bridge communication between *teman tuli* (deaf friends) and non-signers. Our goal was to leverage Foundation Models and CoreML to translate and summarize contextual conversations between Sign Language (**Bisindo**) and spoken text. 

Eventually, we scaled the project down to its core — a streamlined, highly performant sign language learning and recognition app.

## 🤖 Deep Dive: CoreML & Foundation Models

1. **CoreML & CreateML**: Built custom machine learning vision models using CreateML and optimized them through the ML Pipeline to keep on-device inference lightweight and responsive.
2. **Foundation Models**: Designed system prompts to contextualize dialogue between *teman tuli* and non-signers, ensuring natural conversation flow.
3. **Showcase Prototype**: Demonstrated a live two-way translation prototype during the academy showcase to industry experts.
