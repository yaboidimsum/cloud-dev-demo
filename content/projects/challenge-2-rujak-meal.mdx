# Storytelling: Challenge 2 — Daily Annoyances

A daily annoyance didn't seem like much at first. When my team got the chance to explore the daily annoyances of our mentors, I thought that was the problem. Turns out it was just the surface of something bigger.

We split into Desk Research, Observation, and Interview. Using a framework called **R.U.J.A.K** (*Rohani, Uang, Jasmani, Asmara, Keluarga*), we explored every part of our user's life to find where the real problem lived. I learned that a good interview doesn't need to be rigid — just guiding questions, and letting it flow like a natural conversation.

What started as a vague annoyance narrowed into this: our user had been eating the exact same oatmeal every lunch for 5 years. She didn't need a brand new meal; she just needed it to feel less repetitive. 

So we built an iOS app that gives her flavor variations and highlights the health benefits of what she's already eating.
